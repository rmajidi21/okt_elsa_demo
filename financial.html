Model conventions: pilot revenue is recognized in the year the pilot is signed. Pilots that convert start their annual program the following year. Annual programs renew at the renewal rate each year. Counts are rounded to whole customers at each step and revenue is calculated from those counts. Contracted ARR entering 2030 counts 2029 renewals plus conversions from 2029 pilots at list price.

Sources: OKT pricing and value summary; OKT pro pilot timeline and milestone guide; OKT investor deck, September 2026. Injury figures from a retrospective 11-season analysis of shoulder and elbow injuries in MLB pitchers, 2015 to 2025, inflation-adjusted to 2025 USD.

Forward-looking figures are projections. Gross margin is a target pending the COGS build. The readiness score is decision support for sports medicine staff and does not diagnose or replace clinical judgment. Implied burn is the round divided by the stated runway.
